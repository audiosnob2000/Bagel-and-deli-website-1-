The Shop

A deli that keeps commuters' hours.

Route 25A is dark when the lights come on inside. Contractors, night-shift crews, and early commuters make up the first rush — the counter knows them by their name and their order.

Bagels are boiled fresh daily, the deli case is stocked with Boar's Head, and the griddle runs non-stop through the morning.

Open before the sun, closed by mid-afternoon — built entirely around the hours people actually need it. Miller Place Bagels & Deli
